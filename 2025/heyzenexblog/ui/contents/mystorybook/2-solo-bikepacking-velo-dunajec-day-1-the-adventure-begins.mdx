<Section text="The Beginning" />
Today was the day. After almost half a year of preparation, it was hard to
believe it was finally happening. I’d been dreaming of this trip for two years,
but fear had always held me back.

The idea of being far from home, venturing into unfamiliar territory, feels intimidating at first. I’d be lying if I said I wasn’t scared and worried about everything—but I was. What I realized later, though, is that those fears faded the moment I allowed myself to enjoy the ride, the scenery, and the adventure.

I caught a train to Zakopane from Wrocław at 8:47 and arrived at 15:47, spending eight hours watching the landscape change through the window. From there, I set off on the Velo Dunajec route. The real magic begins after about 27 km: the panoramic views of mountains, valleys, and the river are breathtaking.

<PhoCol photo={photoExamples} />

After around 50 km of riding, I reached the Frydman campsite just after 7 pm. There was no space left, but the manager kindly suggested I look around for a spot. Five minutes later, I found a quiet, perfect place right by the river. I was relieved. The next campsite would have been another 20 km away, with long uphill sections, and it was already dark.
