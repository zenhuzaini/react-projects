Today I went bikepacking for the first time, and honestly, it was one of the best experiences ever. I didn’t have any proper gear, so I only brought some clothes and food. Luckily, my friend lent me a tent and a sleeping pad.

It was just a short overnight trip with a group of about seven people. We camped at Virgin Lake, which is around 20 km from home.

The lake is surrounded by a huge forest, and it’s a great place to camp because wild camping is allowed there by the government. There was also a cute doggo hanging around with us during the trip, which somehow made the whole vibe feel even more wholesome 😄
