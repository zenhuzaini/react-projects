My plan was to ride from Frydman to Nowy Sącz and spend the night in a campsite there.
I set off at 7:30, and once again the Velo Dunajec route proved itself: the views are simply incomparable.

The road from Frydman runs long and smooth alongside the Dunajec River. Watching
the river glide under the soft morning light left me completely speechless. The
scenery felt almost unreal, and I honestly couldn’t find the right words to
describe how beautiful it was. Riding in the cool morning air gave me a special
kind of energy and an uplifting mood that carried me through the day.

After the river path ended, I faced one brutal climb. 😂 But as the law of gravity says: whatever goes up must come down. When I finally reached the top, I was blown away by the view. It was absolutely incredible. Once again, I was quiet, partly because I was trying to catch my breath. 😅 The panorama, though, was pure magnificence.

Then came the descent. As I picked up speed, the landscape greeted me again with rolling hills and a shimmering lake—totally majestic. Just… dang. Awesome.

<PhoCol photo={photoExamples} />
Shortly after, I rode through forests and open fields. Another climb waited for
me, but this one didn’t bother me much. The view from the top was once again
breathtaking.

The day was supposed to be hot, but I got lucky: the sky remained cloudy, and a gentle chill kept the temperature pleasant. I’m grateful for that.

Along the way, I kept meeting more bikepackers. We exchanged smiles, quick waves, and sometimes brief chats. At one point, I stopped at a grocery store to refuel and ended up speaking with an older Polish couple. I didn’t even realize how much Polish I could actually speak until that moment. When it really mattered, the words flowed almost naturally. We parted ways with the hope of running into each other again in Nowy Sącz, but it never happened.
